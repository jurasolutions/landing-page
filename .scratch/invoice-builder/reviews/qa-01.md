### Risks R1 to R5

- **R1 wrong money:** passes, except that an amount discount over the subtotal (or over its line) is
  clamped with no error. See **defect 03**. A blank, non-numeric, negative or over-100 GST rate is listed as
  missing and makes no file (exploratory: "", "abc", "-9", "101", "9%"). A rate of "0" with GST on is
  accepted and prints "GST (0%) 0.00" on a tax invoice. The test `required.test.js` > "accepts a GST rate of
  0 and of 8.5" pins this as intended. Zero-rated supplies are out of scope (PRD §3), so this is noted, not
  filed.
- **R2 IRAS items:** passes, except for defect 02.
- **R3 data loss from a file:** passes for every crafted file tried: `gst.on: "true"` opens as GST off;
  unknown keys are dropped; 201 items and 1,001-character payment details are refused; a missing customer
  opens with an empty customer. One note, not filed: the reader takes "2026-02-30" as a date, which only a
  hand-edited file can carry, and the quote builder's reader behaves the same.
- **R4 privacy:** passes. `offline.test.js` (no request while building, saving or remembering; no network
  or storage code of its own); the entry chunk is free of pdf-lib.
- **R5 quote-builder regressions:** every quote-builder test passes (apart from the one-off flake, defect 04).

## Defects

Each has a repro, and a test that goes red when the defect is fixed. Repros are given here and not filed
as `issues/` tickets, as the QA brief asked.

### 01. A bad file removes the "prices include GST" warning (minor)

- **Steps:** open a quote file saved with GST on and "Prices include GST" ticked (UAT `quote-Q-incl.quote.json`).
  The D6 warning shows. Then pick any bad file, such as UAT `bad.json`.
- **Expected:** the bad-file message shows, and nothing else on the page changes (PRD §2 criterion 6), so
  the D6 warning stays while the GST-inclusive prices are still on the invoice.
- **Actual:** the warning disappears and only the bad-file message shows. The invoice still has the
  inclusive prices with GST added on top, so a visitor who missed the warning the first time may now issue
  a tax invoice that charges GST twice. `FileActions.jsx:51` calls `setNotice(null)` before reading the
  file, and then `setNotice("bad")` replaces it.
- **Test:** `tests/invoice-builder-files-wiring.test.js` > "DEFECT 01: keeps the D6 warning when a bad file
  is picked after an inclusive quote" (`it.fails`).
- **Note:** D6 says the warning stays "until the next file or New invoice". This reading treats a refused
  file as no file, because criterion 6 says a bad file changes nothing. If the conductor reads D6 the other
  way, close this as intended and delete the test.

### 02. A required field that prints as nothing passes the tax-invoice check (major)

- **Steps:** with GST on and every field filled, type the customer name as "陈氏贸易" and the customer
  address as "新加坡". Click "Download PDF".
- **Expected:** a tax invoice is not made without the customer's name and address (PRD §2 criterion 4, the
  IRAS list in §4). The field is listed as missing, as an empty one is.
- **Actual:** the warning "Some characters can't go into the PDF, so they will be left out: ..." shows,
  `missingFields` returns `[]`, and the PDF downloads titled "Tax invoice" with no customer name or address.
  The line under "Bill to" is the contact name. The same happens with the business name, the business
  address and an item's description. Customers with Chinese-only names are common in Singapore.
  `required.js` checks `blank(value)` on the raw text, while `pdf.js` prints `toPrintable(value)`.
- **Tests:** `tests/invoice-builder-qa-defects.test.js` > "DEFECT 02 ..." (1 test pinning today's
  behaviour, plus 5 `it.fails` tests, one per field).
- **Severity:** major under exit criterion 6 ("any missing IRAS item on a tax invoice"). The on-page
  warning lowers the risk but does not stop the file. A likely fix is for `required.js` to treat a field
  whose `toPrintable` text is blank as missing. That is the developer's call.

### 03. An amount discount larger than what it comes off is clamped silently (minor)

- **Steps:** add one line, 1 x 100.00. Set the overall discount to the amount 500 (or set the line's
  discount to the amount 500).
- **Expected:** an inline error, and no file while it shows, as for a percentage over 100
  (`test-strategy.md` R1: "a discount over 100% or over the subtotal is clamped and flagged").
- **Actual:** no error. The discount is clamped to 100.00, the total is 0.00, and "Download PDF" makes the
  file. A typo such as 500 for 50.0 gives a zero invoice without a word. The maths is the shared `calc.js`,
  so the quote builder behaves the same way, and any fix there must keep every quote-builder test passing
  (R5).
- **Tests:** `tests/invoice-builder-qa-defects.test.js` > "DEFECT 03 ..." (2 `it.fails`, 1 pinning today's
  behaviour, 1 comparison that passes).

### 04. Test flake in the quote builder's inclusive-GST page-edge test (minor, not this feature)

- **Seen:** the first `npx vitest run` after `npm ci` failed one test:
  `src/apps/quote-builder/gst-inclusive.test.js` > "the PDF with prices that include GST > keeps the total,
  the split and the statement together on one page at every page edge". The next 8 full runs, 2 of them with
  a cleared cache, passed. Run alone it takes 339 ms.
- **Likely cause (not confirmed):** the test builds 25 PDFs in one `it`, under Vitest's default 5 s timeout,
  during a cold run where module import took 48 s in total across the workers. The failure text was not
  captured.
- **Origin:** the file is on `main` (commits 6a7806e and 74bd311) and is not changed by this feature.
- **Test:** none. A flake has no deterministic red step. If it recurs, record the error, then give the
  test an explicit timeout or split the loop. **Owner:** the developer, at the next touch of the quote
  builder.
