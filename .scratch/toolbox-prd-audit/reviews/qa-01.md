# QA 01: the toolbox, end to end, against the PRDs

Branch tested: `crew/toolbox-prd-audit` at `cffc021` (production is `main` at `619e252`).
Run on 2026-10-25 by the QA engineer, driving the built site in Chromium through Playwright.

Verdict: the live tools (quote builder, PDF merge and split) and the site pieces meet their
acceptance criteria. One defect matters for the receipt reader before it goes live (defect 1,
major). It is a draft tool, so it does not affect what this branch ships. The rest are minor:
keyboard focus, one missing `aria-invalid`, input padding, and a two-tab edge case.

Evidence (screenshots, downloads, fixtures, stub, scripts) is in the QA scratchpad, not the
repo. `$QA` below means
`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-tingy-OneDrive-Desktop-projects-jurasolutions-repos-landing-page/a7357aa2-31be-4a6d-aaac-2b12a245f0da/scratchpad/qa`.

lists `.dev.vars` as read-only for the crew. Nothing was written to `.dev.vars`.

Nothing real was called. Anthropic and Turnstile siteverify went to `$QA/stub.mjs` on

locally by Playwright with `$QA/fake-turnstile.js`. Resend was never reached: with no
`RESEND_API_KEY`, `/submit` answers 500 before any fetch. The only third-party requests were
the site's Google Fonts, as on production.

Fixtures were made with pdf-lib in `$QA/make-fixtures.mjs`. They include two genuinely
encrypted PDFs (RC4, Standard security handler R2, written by hand: one with a user password,
one with an owner password only). pdf.js opens both with the right password, which confirms
the encryption is real.

## Defects

Each has a ticket in `issues/`. Severity: major = wrong or misleading behaviour a visitor
meets; minor = an accessibility or polish gap with a workaround.

1. **The receipt reader says "This try wasn't counted" when it was.** Major (draft tool; fix
   before APP-002 goes live). Ticket `issues/06-defect-try-message-claims-not-counted.md`.
   Failing test: `tests/try-message.test.js` (3 red).
2. **Focus falls to the page body after a busy action.** Minor. Merge and download, Download
   selected pages, Enter in Pages to keep, Split into single pages and the quote builder's
   Download PDF all disable themselves while working, so keyboard focus drops to `<body>`.
   Ticket `issues/07-defect-focus-lost-after-busy-action.md`. No unit seam (no DOM in the
   suite); Playwright repro in the ticket.
3. **Quote builder: moving an item to the top or bottom drops focus.** Minor. Ticket
   `issues/08-defect-quote-item-move-loses-focus.md`. No unit seam; repro in the ticket.
4. **APP-003 "Pages to keep" error is not exposed as invalid.** Minor. Ticket
   `issues/09-defect-pages-to-keep-no-aria-invalid.md`.
5. **APP-003 text inputs have 2 px left padding.** Minor, visual. Ticket
   `issues/10-defect-app-003-input-padding.md`.
6. **A second tab erases the remembered business details.** Minor. Ticket
   `issues/11-defect-stale-tab-erases-remembered-details.md`.

## Exploratory charter (30 minutes, beyond the scripted cases)

Charter: odd inputs, back button, refresh mid-flow, slow network, two tabs, a narrow phone.

- **Odd files:** a Unicode, space and `#` file name (`报价 单 #1.pdf`) merges and lists fine. An
  empty file is called "not a PDF". An **owner-password-only PDF**, which opens in any reader
  without a password, is told "This PDF is password protected. Remove the password in your
  PDF reader…". That is accurate to pdf-lib's refusal but may confuse a visitor who never
  typed a password. Observation; editing the words is a product call.
- **Back button:** leaving the quote builder by "All tools" and coming back loses the quote
  with no warning (no `beforeunload` or in-app prompt). Observation: the PRD offers Save
  quote file and asks for no guard.
- **Refresh mid-flow:** the PDF tool comes back empty on the Merge tab, as PRD §5.3 intends
  (files dropped on page leave). Both panels keep their files while switching tabs.
- **Slow network (1.5 s latency, 50 KB/s):** the PDF tool takes about 9 s to become
  interactive. Files added at once show "Checking the file…" and settle by about 14 s.
  Offline after load, merge and split still work; only the usage beacons fail.
- **Two tabs:** defect 6.
- **Narrow phone (360 px):** no horizontal scroll on `/apps`, all three tools, `/privacy` or
  `/unsubscribed`. The site header's "Open menu" button is 42 × 42 (the design system's
  IconButton default size; it belongs with deferred finding 16 in the design system, not this
  repo). Observation.
- **Other observations, not defects:**
  - `/unsubscribed` with no or an unknown `status` says "You're unsubscribed" even if nothing
    happened (only reachable by typing the URL).
  - `/apps` has a visually hidden h2 that repeats the h1 ("Free tools for the paperwork"), so
    a screen reader hears it twice.
  - The quote builder's "Start a new quote" dialog puts initial focus on the clearing action
    rather than "Keep editing".
  - The home page's enquiry form inputs have the same 2 px left padding as defect 5. That
    form predates the toolbox and is on production today.
- **Console:** no script errors or warnings across the session. The only errors were the
  expected failed requests: `/submit` 500 (no key), and a 403 and a 429 from requests I made
  by hand.

## Left untested, and why

- Real Anthropic output, real Turnstile, real Resend sends: forbidden here, and stubbed.
- Safari, Firefox and Acrobat rendering of the PDFs: Chromium only in this run. The quote
  PDF and merged files were checked with pdf.js.
- Excel and Google Sheets opening the exports: the files' structure was checked, not opened
  in those apps.
- `jura-growth check-copy`: not rerun (no copy changed since the developer and reviewer runs).
