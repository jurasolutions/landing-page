# QA-01: APP-004 Product photo studio

*QA engineer, 2026-10-27. Branch `crew/food-post-maker` at 4ab403e. HEAD moved to 59fc33c during the
run, and that commit changes only `README.md` outside `.scratch/`. Spec: PRD
`jurasolutions/prd/pending/apps/prd-app-004-product-photo-studio-2026-10-27.md` §2, with
`reviews/direction-change.md` winning where they differ. Strategy: `reviews/test-strategy.md`,

downloading (`issues/04-defect-desktop-share-sheet.md`, major). Criteria 3 and 7 depend on what the
models do, so only their server-side guards are checked here. The rest belongs to the live check.

**Browser driving.** Playwright MCP (Chromium) at 1280 x 900 and 360 x 780, plus a touch context
(360 px, `hasTouch`, `isMobile`, Android user agent) for the camera button. Nothing reached
Anthropic, Turnstile or Workers AI. The stubs were set up as follows:

- `challenges.cloudflare.com/**`: a fake `window.turnstile` that renders and resolves `execute()`
  with `qa-fake-token`.
- `/api/apps/limit`: answers whatever the case sets: `{ triesLeft, capReached, triesPerDay, ready }`.
- `/api/apps/usage`: 204.
- `POST /api/apps/product-photo-studio`: records the multipart body (counts, filenames, types and
  SOF dimensions of `files` and `refs`, plus `token`, `name` and `instruction`), waits 0.3 to 2.5 s,
  then answers as set out in `server/product-photo-studio.js`. For a 200, each `image` is a real
  1024 x 1280 JPEG drawn on a canvas, with a red band at the top and a blue band at the bottom so
  that a crop shows. Statuses 422, 429 (`limit`, `cap`), 502, 503 (with and without `triesLeft`),
  403, 413 and 415 (`heic`) were each answered with their contract body. A network failure came
  from `route.abort("internetdisconnected")`.
- Input photos were made locally as PNG and converted with `sips`: a 2400 x 1600 JPG, a 1200 x 1600
  JPG, an 800 x 800 PNG, a 640 x 480 JPG and a 900 x 1200 JPG. There was also a real HEIC
  (`sips -s format heic`), a 7-byte "JPG" of garbage and a `.txt` file.

Screenshots and downloaded files are in `.scratch/food-post-maker/qa/` (numbered below).
